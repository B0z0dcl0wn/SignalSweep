SignalSweep Documentation

Mode 1: Beacon Bandit

Anti-Stalking & Spoofing: Sweeps for personal Bluetooth trackers including Apple AirTags, Samsung SmartTags, Tile, and Chipolo devices. Protects against unwanted stalking.
Includes an active Spoofing Engine that blasts BLE pairing requests for Apple and Samsung devices for pentesting and alerts.

Mode 2: Watchers Watch

Surveillance Mapping: Actively hunts for hidden surveillance technology. Detects Flock Safety ALPRs (Solar Penguin Packs) via Random Static Address fingerprints, and Axon Enterprise Body Cameras / TASER Signal triggers (UUIDs fe6c/fe6d).

Mode 3: Sky Sweeper

Airspace Defense: Pocket AWACS drone sniffer. Leverages ASTM F3411 Remote ID (Wi-Fi NAN/Beacon + BLE) to capture real-time drone telemetry, altitude, speed, and the pilot's exact GPS coordinates.
