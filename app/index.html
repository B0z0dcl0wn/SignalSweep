Site Survey

Stand next to a suspect device, capture the raw air, and the app tells you its signature — then log it (photo + GPS) to your encrypted database and export it for DeFlock. Captures need the USB cable, or an SD card in the board.

1 · Investigate an environment

Logs every WiFi frame and BLE advert on all channels. Longer is better — one full sweep takes a few minutes, so give it time to catch a rare transmitter. The detector's beeper is paused while capturing.

Connect the board by USB cable, or fit an SD card — a capture is far too much data for Bluetooth.

One store, one key: this deletes every pin, every logged find and its photos. It cannot be undone, and a forgotten PIN is the same thing — by design.

Detection

Attack‑gear detection flags nearby hacking tools — a Pwnagotchi, a Flipper Zero, an OUI‑Spy or a WiFi Pineapple’s control network, plus deauth bursts, karma/PineAP access points and BLE popup‑spam floods. Off by default: it is attack‑adjacent and some of it trips in busy places. These devices list and beep under All only, never the camera tab. Persisted on the board.

Emissions

Receive-only stops the device announcing itself — no advertising, no connection. It keeps scanning and keeps beeping; this is not the buzzer mute. Over Bluetooth this connection will drop — tap the BOOT button on the device to bring it back for two minutes. Over a USB cable nothing drops: only the radio goes quiet, and you can turn it back on here.
