How SignalSweep works

Turn it on, pocket it or dash-mount it. The device scans BLE and WiFi and beeps when it hears known surveillance hardware. You don't need the phone for it to work.

The buzzer tells you what it is

📷 ALPR / camera — two long beeps
🎥 Body cam — long-short-short
🛸 Drone (Remote ID) — rising trill
📍 Tracker — fast ticking

The phone (optional)

Connect for a live view of what's matching right now, to tune the alarm, or to edit the signature list. Nothing is stored when you close it.

Recording is opt-in

Turn Recording on and the app asks, per device, whether to save its location. Those pins are the only thing kept — encrypted behind your PIN, so a lost phone reveals nothing.
