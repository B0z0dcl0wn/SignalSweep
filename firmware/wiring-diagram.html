Wiring the detector

All you need is the board. A bare XIAO ESP32-S3 with its U.FL antenna is a working detector: plug it into USB, connect the app, and it streams everything it hears. The buzzer, the LED bar and the microSD reader are optional add-ons. The microSD wiring is final, but firmware support is coming: the board does not use the card yet.

Reading your board D# = GPIO#

  • Seeed silk-screens the pads D0–D10; the firmware names the same pads by GPIO number. Wire by pad name and one harness fits both the S3 and the C5. On this board: D1 = GPIO2, D2 = GPIO3, D3 = GPIO4, D4 = GPIO5, D5 = GPIO6, D8 = GPIO7, D9 = GPIO8, D10 = GPIO9.
  • One long edge has D0–D6; the other has 5V, GND, 3V3, D10, D9, D8, D7, with the power group next to the USB. The pad printed VUSB on the underside is the same 5V.

Power and ground

GND XIAO GND→bar GND pad 1═bar GND pad 2→SD GND The buzzer − also goes on bar GND pad 1, next to the wire from the XIAO. A bar pad is big enough for two wires; a XIAO pad is not. This assumes the common 4-pad bar (GND, IN, VCC, GND), whose two GND pads are joined on the bar. A 3-pad bar has one GND pad, which would need all three ground wires. No bar at all? Assembly

  1. One wire per XIAO pad. The XIAO's holes are tiny: two wires shoved into one is how a joint cracks. So each XIAO pad gets exactly one wire. Where two wires have to meet (ground), they meet on a bigger LED-bar pad, never on the XIAO. Skip any part you don't have.
  2. Antenna. Press the U.FL antenna onto the XIAO's small antenna socket until it clicks. No soldering.
  3. LED bar (optional). Wire the IN end: the little arrows printed on the bar point away from it.
    • LED bar pad VCC → XIAO pad 5V
    • LED bar GND pad 1 (the GND beside IN) → XIAO pad GND
    • LED bar pad IN → XIAO pad D1 (GPIO2). Optional: a 330–470 Ω resistor in this wire, right at the bar.
  4. Buzzer (optional). A small passive buzzer (<30 mA) runs straight off the pin.
    • Buzzer + → XIAO pad D2 (GPIO3)
    • Buzzer − → LED bar GND pad 1, next to the XIAO's ground wire (a bar pad takes two wires).
  5. SD reader power (optional). 3.3 V ONLY. Never connect this board to 5V: it has no regulator and 5V can kill the card.
    • SD reader pin 3v3 → XIAO pad 3V3 (not 5V)
    • SD reader pin GND → LED bar GND pad 2 (the GND at the far end, beside VCC).
  6. SD reader data (optional). Four wires, each to its own XIAO pad. Match by the label printed on the reader.
    • SD reader pin CS → XIAO pad D3 (GPIO4)
    • SD reader pin MOSI → XIAO pad D10 (GPIO9)
    • SD reader pin CLK → XIAO pad D8 (GPIO7)
    • SD reader pin MISO → XIAO pad D9 (GPIO8): data comes back from the card on this one
    The wiring is final; the firmware does not drive the card yet (support is coming), so a card in the slot does nothing for now.
  7. Check before you power it. Nothing on the SD reader touches 5V: follow its 3v3 wire and make sure it ends on the XIAO's 3V3 pad. Then count: one wire in each XIAO pad, no stray strands bridging two pads, and D4 / D5 left empty.
  8. Verify. Flash from the web flasher, or python flash.py --tier 1 --port COM3. On boot you get a short jingle and an LED blink (if fitted). With no clock fitted the serial log says [RTC] none fitted, waiting for a host, which is expected. Connect the app: Settings › Device identity › Clock shows the phone synced the time. Then trigger a match (an AirTag near it, or edit a signature): the bar flashes and the buzzer plays that category's pattern — ALPR / camera two long beeps, body cam long-short-short, drone a rising trill, tracker fast ticking.

Watch for

Power budget ≈480 mA

  • 8× WS2812 at full white peaks near 480 mA. SignalSweep only flashes short colored bursts, so USB 5V is fine.
  • Add a 1000 µF cap across the bar's 5V↔GND to absorb inrush when all eight snap on.
  • An SD card adds a few mA idle and roughly 100 mA peaks on writes, all from the XIAO's 3V3. On a weak battery, budget for that.

Gotchas clones

  • SD reader on 5V kills the card. This reader has no regulator: 3V3 only.
  • Other SD readers put their pins in a different order, and some want 5V (they have a regulator on board). The drawing is for the 6-pin 3v3, CS, MOSI, CLK, MISO, GND reader; with any other, match by label and check its power pin's marking.
  • 3.3 V data into a 5 V-powered clone bar usually works; if LED 1 flickers, add the 330–470 Ω series resistor at DIN.
  • Wrong end (wiring DOUT) lights nothing — flip to DIN.
  • Active buzzer = one fixed pitch: you'll hear each pattern's rhythm but not its tones. Passive is the intended part.
  • Two wires in one XIAO pad is how a joint cracks. Double up on a bar pad instead.
